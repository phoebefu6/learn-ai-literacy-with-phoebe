✓ What this course is

Understanding you can decide with: what AI, ML, deep learning and GenAI are, how neural nets and embeddings work, recommendations, search, how LLMs work, prompting and RAG, and AI strategy and risk. Everything a leader or practitioner needs to evaluate, decide, and govern AI.

✗ What it is not

No math (backprop, loss functions, linear algebra). No coding with APIs, no training your own models, no MLOps. This is literacy: understand, evaluate, decide, govern - not build. The builder track is a different course.
